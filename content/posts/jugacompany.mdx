When [Sergio](https://x.com/sergioalegrego) invited me to join him in building [JugáTenis](https://jugatenis.com), we quickly realized there were many other amateur athletes we could help find people to play with, arrange matches, and ultimately play more of the sports they love. I told [the start of that story here](/post/juga-tenis).

That's how [JugáPádel](https://jugapadel.app) was born. We launched it recently, and the response confirmed there was something bigger to build.

JugáTenis was starting to feel too small as a brand for what we wanted to create: products that connect people through sports.

So we created [JugáCompany](https://jugacompany.com), the brand that brings all of that work together and under which we'll keep building.

What lives under it today:

- **JugáTenis** and **JugáPádel**: free apps for iOS, Android and web that match amateur players with rivals at their level, with rankings built from match results.
- **Club**: the court booking system for the clubs that host those matches.
- **Profesor+**: connects coaches with students in their area.

We have consolidated operations in Argentina and Uruguay and are expanding across the rest of Latin America and Spain. I'm co-founder, driving growth and expansion beyond the court and looking after the architecture and engineering underneath it.

There's a lot ahead of us, many sports to explore, and most importantly, many people to connect. Because sports are life.

Here it is: [https://jugacompany.com](https://jugacompany.com)
