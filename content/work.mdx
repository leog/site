Fifteen years of building and leading frontend teams, from a software factory to an open source startup and back. Here's a summary of my work so far.

## Cresteo

*Front-end Architect and Team Leader, October 2023 – Present*

At [Cresteo](https://cresteo.com), part of the Wizards group, I work with [Infobase](https://infobase.com)'s CTO and stakeholders on **Musaeum**, a platform that consolidates Infobase's scattered content products into a single multi-entry-point application. Concretely:

- Took over the development team as main architect and redesigned the platform on **Next.js** App Router with React Server Components, **Prisma** on AWS RDS MSSQL, and **SST** deployments to AWS with PR previews, pre-production and production environments.
- Set up monitoring and product analytics with **Sentry**, Google Analytics and Pendo.
- Led the beta release to early users, whose feedback is now driving broader adoption inside Infobase.

## JugáCompany

*Co-founder, 2026 – Present*

With [Sergio Gregorutti](https://x.com/sergioalegrego) I co-founded [JugáCompany](https://jugacompany.com), the brand behind **JugáTenis** and **JugáPádel**: free apps that match amateur players with rivals at their level, plus a booking system for clubs and Profesor+ for coaches. Operating in Argentina and Uruguay, expanding across Latin America and Spain. I drive growth and expansion and look after the architecture and engineering ([the story](/post/jugacompany)).

## Cal.com

*Full Stack Developer, core team, February 2022 – October 2023*

I found [Cal.com](https://cal.com/) while looking for a scheduling tool, started contributing as a side project, and got hired a few months later ([the full story](/post/lucky-stumble-into-open-source)). On the core team I:

- Built **recurring events** for the main booking flow, the biggest feature I shipped there.
- Added and improved integrations in the **app store**.
- Worked in the open with the community, onboarding new contributors and maintaining documentation.
- Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Turborepo, Vercel.
